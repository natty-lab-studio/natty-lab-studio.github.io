1. Scope

Daily Todo stores tasks, categories, routines and profile content primarily on your device. Optional Google Drive sync, Android system backup or device transfer, subscriptions, advertising, visits to this website, and messages you choose to send involve other systems. Natty Lab does not run a separate task database server, but may receive information you email to us and subscription support information.

4. Retention and security

Device content may remain until you delete it or clear app data, subject to system backup copies. Files in your Drive remain under your account until you remove them, subject to Google's trash and retention rules. Natty Lab handles support mail for the request and applicable obligations. Third-party records follow applicable provider practices and legal duties. Clearing the app cannot erase every copy held by Google, GitHub or RevenueCat. Connections to these services use HTTPS.

5. Delete data and exercise your rights

Delete individual items in the app. To clear local data, use Android Settings > Apps > Daily Todo > Storage > Clear data. Uninstalling may leave an OS backup or Drive copy. To remove optional sync files, delete the TodoOnly4Me folder in your Google Drive, empty the trash if appropriate, disconnect Drive in the app and revoke access in Google Account permissions. Turn off sync first to avoid recreating files.

For access, correction or deletion of information Natty Lab can identify and handle, email nattylab.studio@gmail.com. For subscription records, include the support ID from the app's Support settings if available. You may request deletion of emails and attachments you sent us. Do not email passwords, access tokens or card numbers. We may seek reasonable information to verify and locate the record.

For a correction or deletion request covered by Korean law, we will take the required action for information we control and notify you of the result within 10 days of the request, or notify you of the reason for refusal and how to object. This does not promise complete erasure of every external record within 10 days. Legal retention duties and provider-controlled records may remain. Deleting a RevenueCat customer does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Later app use or purchase restoration can create subscription records again. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play.
